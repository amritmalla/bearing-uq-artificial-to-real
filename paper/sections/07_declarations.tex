\section*{Data and code availability}

The Paderborn bearing data are publicly available from the KAt-DataCenter of Paderborn University
\citep{katdatacenter} under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 licence. The code, configuration,
Colab notebooks and all result tables needed to reproduce the figures and tables are available at
\url{https://github.com/amritmalla/bearing-uq-artificial-to-real}. The code is released under the MIT licence.
